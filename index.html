For Heavy Readers & Researchers

Built for keeping, not just skimming.

Most RSS readers are disposable firehoses. Tenwise is a dedicated research environment that turns your reading habit into a compound asset of notes, citations, and synthesized intelligence.

Dense Rows & Docked Reader

Review hundreds of headlines effortlessly in two-line rows with day headings. When you open an article, the docked 3-pane reader delivers full text with beautiful Newsreader serif typography, focus mode (f), and remembered scroll positions.

Save with a Note (n)

Never wonder why you bookmarked an article six months later. Press n to record your reaction right away. The Saved view displays your notes front and center, with an Unsorted queue for quick triage into thematic collections.

Ready-to-Paste APA 7 Citations

Every save automatically formats an APA 7 citation. Hover over any row to copy, or copy an entire collection’s bibliography with one click. Preprints are detected and cited with their arXiv IDs; messy author fields are cleanly formatted.

Private On-Device AI

Summaries, analysis, and article Q&A run on Gemini Nano directly inside Google Chrome or via local Ollama. Your reading text never leaves your device. No API keys, no subscription tiers, and no cloud data leakage.

Your Voice & Multi-Article Actions

Nine reading personas (e.g. Critical Analyst, Skeptic, Executive) shape every AI output. Select 2–15 articles to Compare Sources, generate an Executive Briefing, or compile a Daily Digest exportable to Word or Markdown.

Studio & Send to NotebookLM

Turn any collection into a grounded knowledge source for Google NotebookLM with one click. Or open Studio to format articles into newspaper-style print editions and export to high-res PNG or print-ready A4 PDF.

Unlike other AI readers that transmit your articles to cloud API providers, Tenwise runs AI directly on your own device using desktop Google Chrome’s built-in Gemini Nano model (Chrome Prompt API) or an optional local Ollama instance. Desktop Google Chrome 148+ has built-in Gemini Nano support requiring zero setup or API keys. If you use Safari, Firefox, or other browsers, you can connect a local Ollama instance (Llama 3.2, Gemma, DeepSeek R1) with our built-in detection wizard.
