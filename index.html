From raw feed firehose to cited research and print.

§ 06 — QUESTIONS & ARCHITECTURE

Frequently Asked Questions

Tenwise uses Gemini Nano (built into desktop Google Chrome 148+ via the Prompt API) or connects to a local Ollama server on your machine. Summaries, article Q&A, and multi-article briefings run directly on your hardware—the Tenwise server has no AI model and never sends your reading text to external AI providers.
Tenwise is built on a modular plugin architecture (editorial-ai, studio, notebooklm, custom source connectors). For enterprise publishers, we deploy a dedicated branded workspace (reader.yourbrand.com) integrated with your subscriber SSO, pre-populated with your curated topic dossiers, custom house-style AI prompts, and Studio PDF templates carrying your masthead.
When you sign in at tenwise.app/login (passwordless email code/link), you get an isolated container of your own. Your SQLite database (reader.db) and settings are replicated continuously by Litestream to Cloudflare R2 so your library survives container sleep and redeploys. You can also export OPML, Markdown collections, and CSV highlights at any time.
