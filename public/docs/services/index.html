Privacy

Services that see your data

Tenwise has no advertising, and no analytics or tracking scripts in the app. This website counts page visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, without cookies. These are the services that do see some of your data when you use Tenwise at tenwise.app: what each sees, why, and for how long. When we add one, it's added here first. The Privacy Policy covers the rest.

Services Tenwise uses for everyone

ServiceWhat it seesWhyFor how long
CloudflareEvery request to tenwise.app (address, browser, the page), its logs, and the check at sign-in (Turnstile). Your account: email address, sessions, plan, email preferences, usage counts, Tenwise AI usage (tokens and cost, not text), your answer to the welcome's question, where you came from (a sign-up link's campaign or referral) and any feedback you send. Briefs you share by link, and the briefs a reader account keeps. Your workspace while it runs, and a continuous copy of your library: feeds, articles, notes, highlights, collections, Studio drafts and settings. On this website's pages only (not in the app): Web Analytics, which counts visits by page address, referring site, browser, device type, country and load time, with no cookies.Hosting: sign-in, the app, and keeping your library safe between sessions.Account and library: until you delete your account, or 90 days after a trial or subscription ends without paying. Logs: up to 7 days. Web Analytics: totals only, nothing that identifies you.
Amazon Web Services (Simple Email Service)Your email address and the emails we send you: sign-in codes and links, trial and billing emails, and the daily digest (titles and sources of new articles).Sending email.As AWS keeps sending records for delivery and abuse checks.
StripeYour email address, your payment details (entered on Stripe's own pages; Tenwise never sees card numbers) and your subscription.Payments, invoices and managing your plan.As Stripe must keep payment records by law.

Services you choose

ServiceWhenWhat it sees
Amazon Web Services (Bedrock, for Tenwise AI)When you run an AI action with Tenwise AI, the hosted engine.The text of that action (articles, notes, your prompt), sent by Tenwise through AWS in the United States (requests go from US West, Oregon, and may be served from another US region) to the model the action uses (Amazon Nova, or Anthropic's Claude run by AWS). Bedrock doesn't keep it or use it for training, and the model makers don't see it. Tenwise keeps only the token counts and cost, for your monthly allowance.
Your own AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter or another)When you add your key in Settings → AI engines and run an AI action.The text of that action (articles, notes, your prompt), sent from your workspace with your key, under your agreement with that provider.
Google NewsWhen you follow an interest in the welcome or in Add feeds.The words of the interest, in the feed address your workspace fetches. Not your name or email.
The sites you followWhenever your workspace refreshes a feed or opens an article.A request from Tenwise's servers, not from you.
Gemini Nano in ChromeWhen you choose it as your AI engine.Nothing leaves your computer: the model runs in your browser.

The free, open-source Tenwise Engine runs on your own computer and uses none of the services in the first table.
