The recurring brief

A brief you can stand behind, every week.

A weekly update for your team, a client note, a newsletter. Tenwise remembers the job, drafts from your sources and your own notes, and checks every claim before you send it.

  1. Set it up onceOn a collection: who it's for, the question it answers, how often, and its sections.
  2. Draft from your evidenceFrom the collection's articles and only the notes and highlights you tick. What the sources say, your view and the AI's suggestions stay apart.
  3. Check every claimEach claim is linked to the passage of its source that supports it. Unsupported claims, wrong numbers and conflicting sources are flagged.
  4. Approve and sendApprove a fixed version, then copy it for email, download Markdown or HTML, or share it by link.

AI, three ways

Summaries, chat and drafts, your way.

Pick the AI that fits. You can change it any time in Settings, and every service that sees your text is listed, with what it sees.

Included

Tenwise AI

Ready from your first sign-in, with a monthly allowance in your trial and on Plus. Tenwise sends the text of that action to models it runs on AWS Bedrock; it isn't kept there or used for training, and Tenwise keeps only how much you used.

Your provider

Your own key

Claude, OpenAI, OpenRouter (free models included) or another OpenAI-compatible service. Your key is stored encrypted and never shown again; the text you ask about goes only to that provider.

On your device

Gemini Nano in Chrome

The model built into desktop Chrome runs in your browser, so the text never leaves your computer. Free, with no limit from us.

AI in your voice
