Questions

Frequently asked

What happens when my trial ends?

If you've subscribed, nothing changes. If not, your library becomes read-only: you can read it, export it and download it for 90 days, and subscribe any time to pick up where you left off. After 90 days it's deleted, with emails 30 and 7 days before.

How do I cancel?

In Tenwise, open Settings → Account → Manage billing. That opens Stripe's billing page, where you cancel in one click. You keep your plan until the end of the period you've paid for; then your library becomes read-only for 90 days. More on refunds and cancellation.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, on your first payment: if Tenwise isn't for you, write to ask@tenwise.app within 14 days of it and we refund it in full. The same goes for each yearly renewal. Otherwise we don't refund part-used periods, and cancelling keeps your plan to the end of the period. See Refunds and cancellation.

Can I switch between Reader and Plus?

Yes, any time, in Manage billing. Stripe works out the difference for the rest of your period.

Can I take my library with me?

Always, on any plan, read-only included. Settings → Data → Download your library gives you one file with your feeds, folders, saves, highlights, notes, collections, AI summaries and Studio drafts; it opens in the free, open-source Tenwise Engine. Export your saves and notes gives HTML or Markdown for OneNote, Word, Notion or Obsidian.

Someone shared a brief with me. Do I need to pay?

No. Anyone with the link can read a shared brief without signing in. To keep it, press Keep this brief and sign in with your email: that makes a free reader account, with no trial and no card. Your own 14-day trial starts only when you choose Start your own brief.

What's included in Tenwise AI?

Your trial and Plus include a monthly allowance of summaries, chat answers, brief drafts and evidence checks, run by Tenwise on AWS Bedrock. Settings → Account shows what's left and when it resets. When one kind runs out, use your own key or Gemini Nano in Chrome, which have no Tenwise limit. Reader uses your own key or Gemini Nano.

Is there a free plan?

Not for a hosted workspace: each one runs on its own server while you use it, and that costs us real money. What's free: reader accounts for briefs shared with you, and the open-source Tenwise Engine on your own computer.

Do I need a card to try it?

No. Sign in with your email, and you get 14 days of Plus. You only enter a card, on Stripe's own page, if you subscribe.

Can I sign up a class or a team?

Write to ask@tenwise.app with how many people and what you'd use it for, and we'll set you up.
