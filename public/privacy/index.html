Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

In short

  • We collect what it takes to run your workspace: your email, your library, your plan, and counts of how you use Tenwise. Never which articles you read for analytics or ads.
  • No advertising and no selling data. The app has no analytics or tracking scripts; this website counts page visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies.
  • Your AI text goes only where you choose: AWS (Tenwise AI), your own provider, or nowhere (Gemini Nano on your device).
  • Every service that sees your data is listed, with what it sees.
  • Download your library any time. Ask us to delete your account, and we do.

1. Who we are

CodeStrata, Inc. ("we", "us"), 8026 W Nicolet Ave, Glendale, AZ 85303, runs Tenwise at tenwise.app and is the controller of the personal data described here. This policy covers the Tenwise website, the app, our emails and shared brief pages. It doesn't cover the open-source Tenwise Engine you run yourself, which sends us nothing, or the sites and services you choose to connect.

2. What we collect and why

When you sign in

  • Your email address, to create your account and send you a one-time sign-in code and link. There's no password.
  • A session, kept in a secure cookie for up to 30 days, so you stay signed in on that browser. You can sign out of every device in Settings → Account.
  • Abuse protection. Cloudflare Turnstile checks in your browser that a person, not a script, is signing in. To limit sign-in emails and new trials, we count requests per email address and per network; we store those as keyed hashes, not as addresses, and only for the window they count (15 minutes to a day).

Your workspace

Your library: your feeds and folders, the articles your feeds bring in, the pages you save, your notes, highlights, collections, briefs, Studio drafts, AI summaries and settings. It lives in your own workspace on Cloudflare, which runs only while you use Tenwise, and is continuously backed up to Cloudflare R2 storage. It's private to your account. To bring in articles, your workspace requests the feeds and pages you follow from their sites, as Tenwise rather than as you; a Google News feed's address carries the words of that search.

Your account and plan

Your plan and trial dates, email preferences and digest hour, your answer to the welcome question, where you came from, your Tenwise AI usage, and any feedback you send us. If you subscribe, Stripe gives us your subscription status, the plan and its dates, and a customer reference; your card details stay with Stripe.

Email

We send email through Amazon Simple Email Service (SES): sign-in codes; account, trial and billing emails, which always come; and, if you leave them on, the daily digest (the titles and sources of new articles from your feeds), tips and product news. Every email that isn't about your account has a one-click unsubscribe, and you can change them any time in Settings → Account → Email. To send the digest, Tenwise opens your workspace once a day to check your feeds.

Running the service

Like any website, Cloudflare receives every request, including your IP address, browser and the page asked for. Our logs of these requests are kept for up to 7 days to fix problems and stop abuse.

3. AI and your text

AI runs only when you ask for an AI action (a summary, chat, a brief draft or an evidence check) or turn on background processing. Where your text goes depends on the AI engine you use:

Tenwise AITenwise sends the text of that action (the article, your question, and the notes you tick for a brief) to models it runs on Amazon Web Services' Bedrock service in the United States: Amazon Nova, or Anthropic's Claude run by AWS. AWS doesn't keep it or use it to train models, and the model makers don't receive it. We keep only how much you used (tokens and cost) for your monthly allowance, never the text.
Your own keyYour workspace sends the text of that action directly to the provider you chose (such as Anthropic, OpenAI or OpenRouter), under your agreement with them. Your key is stored encrypted in your workspace and never shown again.
Gemini NanoRuns in desktop Chrome on your computer. The text never leaves your device.

Settings → AI engines shows which engine Tenwise is using. We never use your library or your AI text to train models.

4. Usage counts

To understand whether Tenwise is working for people, and to send trial emails that fit what you've done, we record counts, not content: how many feeds, articles read, saves, highlights, notes, collections, AI summaries and Studio items you have, and which days you opened Tenwise. Your workspace reports these numbers about once a day while it's running. Nothing about which articles, feeds or words you read leaves your workspace for this.

5. The welcome question

When you first open Tenwise, we ask, optionally, what you make from your reading, for whom and how often. You pick from fixed choices, with no free text. We store your answer on your account to set up your first brief and to learn, in totals, which kinds of work Tenwise serves.

6. Where you came from

If you sign up from a link that names a campaign, an edition or a referral (for example a link on this website, in a newsletter, from a trial code, or from a brief someone shared), we record that name on your account once, when it's created. We use it, in totals, to see which channels bring people who stay. We don't use advertising cookies or pixels for this.

7. Website analytics

Cloudflare, which hosts Tenwise, adds its Web Analytics script to the pages of this website, including the guide. It counts visits to each page: the page's address, the site that linked to it, your browser, operating system and type of device, your country, and how fast the page loaded. It uses no cookies or local storage and doesn't fingerprint you, so it can't recognise you from one visit to the next or follow you to other sites. We see only totals.

It doesn't run in the app, on the sign-in and billing pages, or on shared briefs: their security policy allows only Tenwise's own scripts. If you block it in your browser, the website works the same.

8. Shared briefs and reader accounts

  • Sharing a brief. When you share an approved brief by link, we keep a copy of that version (its text and source links, without your private notes or the draft's marks) and show it to anyone who has the link, without signing in. It shows a byline only if you add one. Stop sharing removes it for everyone. Sign-ups from your link are counted under your referral.
  • Reader accounts. If you keep a brief someone shared, we store your email and the briefs you kept. A reader account has no workspace, and we email it only to sign in.

9. What we don't do

  • We don't show ads, and we don't sell or rent personal data, or "share" it for cross-context advertising.
  • We don't use advertising pixels or tracking cookies anywhere, or analytics or tracking scripts in the app. This website's only analytics is Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page visits without cookies.
  • We don't read your library, except when you ask us to help with a problem, or when the law requires it.
  • We don't use your content to train AI models.

10. Services that process data for us

We use a small number of providers (sub-processors), each only for the purpose listed. Services that see your data lists every one, what it sees and for how long, and we update it before we add one.

CloudflareHosting: the website, sign-in (including Turnstile), the app, your workspace, the account database (D1) and library backups (R2); and Web Analytics on the website's pages.
Amazon Web ServicesEmail (SES) and, when you use it, Tenwise AI (Bedrock), in the United States.
StripePayments, invoices, the billing page and tax calculation. Stripe is also an independent controller for payment data.

Services you choose yourself, such as your own AI provider, Google News feeds, or the sites you follow, receive only what's needed for that request.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, to protect the rights and safety of people or the service, or as part of a merger or sale of the business, in which case this policy continues to apply to it.

11. Cookies and local storage

We use only what the service needs:

  • Two sign-in cookies: your session, and a short-lived one while a sign-in is pending. They can only be read by tenwise.app, not by scripts or other sites.
  • Your browser's local storage, for preferences such as your theme and, in the app, your reading settings on that browser.

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies; Cloudflare Web Analytics on this website uses none. So there's no cookie banner and nothing to opt out of.

13. Export and deletion

  • Download your library: Settings → Data → Download your library, on any plan, read-only included. It opens in the free Tenwise Engine.
  • Export your saves and notes as HTML or Markdown, your feeds as OPML and your highlights as CSV.
  • Delete your account: write to ask@tenwise.app from your account's email address. We delete your library, its backups and every record about you in our account database within 30 days, apart from payment records we must keep. Cancel any subscription first, in Manage billing.

14. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to stop sending you non-essential email. Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, the UK, or California and other US states), you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and not to be treated differently for using your rights. Write to ask@tenwise.app; we'll answer within 30 days and may need to confirm it's you. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

15. Legal bases (EU and UK)

  • Contract: running your account, workspace, sign-in, billing, AI actions you request, sharing you choose, and account emails.
  • Legitimate interests: preventing abuse and fraud, keeping the service secure, usage counts, the welcome question, where you came from and website analytics (to improve Tenwise), and tips and product news, which you can turn off any time.
  • Consent: the daily digest you choose to receive, where the law requires consent.
  • Legal obligation: keeping payment and tax records.

16. International transfers

CodeStrata is in the United States, and Tenwise is run on servers there and in Cloudflare's global network. When we transfer personal data from the EU, the UK or Switzerland, we rely on our providers' commitments under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and its UK and Swiss extensions where they apply, and on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

17. Security

Sign-in uses one-time codes and secure, HTTP-only cookies; secrets are stored as keyed hashes; each person's workspace is kept apart from everyone else's; and connections are encrypted. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach that affects you, we'll tell you as the law requires. To report a security issue, write to ask@tenwise.app with "Security" in the subject.

18. Children

Tenwise is not for children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has an account, write to us and we'll delete it.

19. Changes to this policy

We'll update this policy, and the services list, before we change what we collect or who processes it. If a change is significant, we'll tell you by email or in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top shows the last change.
