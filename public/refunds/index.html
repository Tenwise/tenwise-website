Billing

Refunds and cancellation

Last updated 30 October 2026

In short

  • Cancel any time in Manage billing. No call, no email needed.
  • You keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Changed your mind? We refund your first payment, and any yearly renewal, in full if you ask within 14 days.

How to cancel

  1. In Tenwise, click your initial at the bottom of the left rail, then Plan & billing (or go to Manage billing).
  2. Press Manage billing. Stripe's billing page opens.
  3. Choose Cancel plan and confirm. You'll see the date your plan ends, and Stripe emails you a confirmation.

In the same billing page you can change your card, switch plans and download invoices. If you can't sign in, write to support@tenwise.app from your account's email address and we'll cancel for you.

After you cancel

  • You keep everything in your plan until the end of the period you've paid for. Nothing renews after that.
  • Then your library becomes read-only for 90 days: you can read it, export it and download it, and subscribe again any time to pick up where you left off.
  • After 90 days, your library and account are deleted. We email you 30 days and 7 days before.
  • You can undo a cancellation in the billing page until your period ends.

Refunds

Your first paymentA full refund if you ask within 14 days of it, monthly or yearly. No questions asked.
A yearly renewalA full refund if you ask within 14 days of the renewal charge. We email you before each yearly renewal.
Monthly renewalsNot refunded. Cancel any time and you keep your plan to the end of the month you paid for.
Part-used periodsNot refunded when you cancel, except as described above or where the law requires it.
Our mistakesIf we charged you wrongly, or Tenwise was unavailable for a long time, we'll put it right, with a refund or credit.

To ask for a refund, write to support@tenwise.app from your account's email address. Refunds go back to the card you paid with, through Stripe, and usually arrive within 5 to 10 business days. When we refund a payment, the plan it paid for ends, and your library becomes read-only as described above.

If you're a consumer in the EU or the UK, these refunds cover your legal right to withdraw from the contract within 14 days. Nothing here limits any other right you have under the law where you live.

Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails, Stripe tries again over the next few days and emails you to update your card. You keep full access for up to 7 days. If the payment still hasn't gone through, your library becomes read-only until you pay; update your card in Manage billing and everything works again.

During the trial

There's nothing to cancel. The trial needs no card, so nothing is charged. If you don't subscribe, your library becomes read-only when the trial ends.

Contact support

Write to support@tenwise.app. Please send it from your account's email address and tell us what you were doing and what you saw. We answer within one business day.
