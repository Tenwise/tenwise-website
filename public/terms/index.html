6. Subscriptions and automatic renewal

  • Plans and prices are shown on the pricing page and at checkout. Prices are in US dollars. Taxes such as sales tax or VAT are added where the law requires.
  • Payments are processed by Stripe. You enter your card on Stripe's pages; we never see or store your full card number. Stripe's terms also apply to your payment.
  • Automatic renewal. A subscription renews automatically at the end of each monthly or yearly period, and Stripe charges your payment method for the next period, until you cancel. We'll remind you before a yearly renewal.
  • Failed payments. If a renewal payment fails, Stripe retries it and you keep full access for up to 7 days. If it still fails, your library becomes read-only until you pay.
  • Changing plans. You can switch plans in the billing page; Stripe works out any difference for the rest of the period.
  • Price changes. If we change the price of your plan, we'll tell you by email at least 30 days before it applies to you, and it applies from your next renewal. You can cancel before then.
  • Promotion codes apply as described when you use them, and can't be exchanged for cash.

7. Cancelling

You can cancel any time in Tenwise, under Settings → Account → Manage billing, which opens Stripe's billing page. Cancelling stops the next renewal. You keep your plan until the end of the period you've already paid for; then your library becomes read-only. See Refunds and cancellation.

8. Refunds

If you ask within 14 days of your first payment for a plan, or of a yearly renewal, we'll refund that payment in full. Otherwise, payments are non-refundable and we don't refund part-used periods, except where the law requires it or where we've made a billing mistake. Our refund policy has the details. Nothing in these terms limits a right to cancel or to a refund that you have under the law where you live.

9. Read-only and deletion

When a trial or a paid period ends without payment, your account becomes read-only. You can still read your library, mark articles read, export your saves and notes, and download your whole library. Adding feeds, refreshing, saving, highlighting, AI, briefs and Studio are paused until you subscribe.

We keep a read-only library for 90 days. We email you 30 days and 7 days before it's deleted. After 90 days we delete your library, its backups and your account, except records we must keep by law (see the Privacy Policy). Subscribing within the 90 days restores full access.

Some sites don't allow their articles to be fetched; for those, Tenwise shows what the feed provides and links to the original. Tenwise doesn't try to get around a site's paywall or bot protection.

Your use of publishers' content is your responsibility, including any quoting and sharing. If you're a publisher with a concern, write to ask@tenwise.app.

  • When you share an approved brief by link, anyone with the link can read it, without signing in. Don't share a brief that contains confidential or personal information you aren't allowed to make public.
  • A shared brief shows your text and links to its sources. Keep quotations short, each with its source and a link, and never reproduce whole articles. We may limit how much source text a shared brief shows.
  • You're responsible for what you share, including your claims about others and any quotations.
  • You can stop sharing at any time; the page then stops working for everyone. People who kept the brief will see that it's no longer shared.
  • We may take down a shared brief that breaks these terms or the law, or in response to a valid legal complaint.

13. Reader accounts

If you open a shared brief, you can keep it with a free reader account. A reader account has no workspace and no trial, and we only email it to sign in. It's free for as long as we offer it; we may change or end free reader accounts with notice. Choosing to start your own workspace starts your 14-day trial.

14. AI features and output

  • Output can be wrong. AI can misread sources, miss context, or state things that aren't true. Tenwise links each claim in a brief to the passage it relies on and flags claims it can't support or that sources contradict, but those checks can also be wrong or incomplete. Read the sources and check what matters before you rely on or share anything AI helped write. You're responsible for what you send.

  • As between you and us, you may use the output you generate, subject to the rights of the sources it draws on.
